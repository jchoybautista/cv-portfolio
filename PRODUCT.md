# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, creative directors, founders, and senior product leads evaluating a senior UI/UX designer & front-end developer. They arrive with a specific brief in mind — finding someone who can own the full design-to-code pipeline — and want evidence fast: real work, real craft, real technical depth.

## Product Purpose

Jonathan Bautista's personal portfolio. 13+ years of design and front-end work in one place. The site is the product: it demonstrates the skill it claims, combining editorial visual design with hand-coded motion and interaction. Success means a visitor finishes the scroll knowing Jonathan can lead design AND ship the front end.

## Brand Personality

Bold, precise, cinematic. Dark-native aesthetic inspired by Lando Norris's personal site — neon-lime accent on deep near-black, animated signature reveal, scrollytelling timeline, horizontal work gallery. The design earns attention by being better than a template, not by being louder.

## Anti-references

- Standard Webflow/Framer portfolio templates (grid cards, pastel surfaces, same hero layout as every other designer)
- "Clean minimal" white-page portfolios that prove nothing about craft
- Dribbble-style shot galleries without editorial intent

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — the site's code quality and motion are evidence of the work, not decoration around it.
2. **Show, don't list** — every section reveals competence through execution, not bullet points.
3. **Restraint earns impact** — one accent color, one decorative font; everything else is discipline.
4. **Motion is narrative** — animations tell the story of each section rather than dressing it up.
5. **Mobile is not an afterthought** — responsive behavior is built into every component from the start.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (animations replaced with crossfades or instant transitions). Sufficient contrast on all body copy vs backgrounds.
