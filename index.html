I've spent 13+ years as a Senior UI/UX Designer turning messy ideas into products people actually want to use. I handle the whole cycle from initial research and wireframes to final, high-fidelity prototypes. I've recently added tools like Figma Make and Claude Code to my workflow; they help me speed up the design-to-code process, so I can spend more time focusing on the user experience while I handle the technical implementation.

My portfolio includes work for major brands like AEON Cambodia, Travelbook PH, Content House Inc., Angel's Pizza, Figaro Coffee, and Yamaha Motors Philippines.

Beyond design, I'm a Front-End Developer who focuses on shipping critical, responsive web projects. I led the mobile-web transition for Smart Communications and Talk 'N Text, and I've built front-end architecture for platforms like beep™, Visor PH, East West Bank, Camel Appliances, It's More Fun in the Philippines, POEA, PRU Life UK, and Bonakid.

This dual background, knowing how to design and how to code, means I can bridge the gap between a vision and the actual build to deliver a better end result for the user.
