I've spent 13+ years as a Senior UI/UX Designer, tackling the messiest ideas and sculpting them into digital products people genuinely enjoy using. I manage the whole process, from initial research and wireframes all the way through to polished, high-fidelity prototypes, then translate it perfectly into code.

My comprehensive portfolio includes UI/UX Designs for websites and mobile applications for major brands such as AEON Cambodia, Travelbook PH, Content House Inc., Angel's Pizza, Figaro Coffee, Yamaha Motors Philippines, and more.

In addition to design, as a Front-End Developer, I delivered critical responsive web projects. I pioneered the transition of Smart Communications and Talk 'N Text into fully optimized mobile-web responsive experiences, and developed the front-end architecture for platforms such as beep™, Visor PH, East West Bank, Camel Appliances, It's More Fun in the Philippines, POEA, PRU Life UK, Bonakid, and more.

This dual expertise ensures seamless execution, bridging the gap between design vision and technical implementation to deliver superior user experiences.
